How I Work

End-to-End Production

Everything from first draft to final published frame — handled in-house.

Step 01

Story First

Every project starts with narrative structure: acts, characters, arcs, and themes. The visuals serve the story, not the other way around.

Step 02

Script & Prompt

Scene scripts are written and broken down into generatable prompts — each calibrated for tone, lighting, action, and continuity across clips.

Step 03

Generate & Direct

AI video tools are used as a production pipeline, not a shortcut. Sequences are iterated, rejected, and refined until they match the vision.

Step 04

Edit & Ship

Final cuts are assembled, scored with original AI music, and published across platforms — with the web hub updated to reflect each release.
